The lecture ?Microcontroller? deals with 8- and 16-bit microcontrollers. After a short overview about microprocessors, signal processors and of course microcontrollers we will focus on the special features of microcontrollers. This means that we will try to understand why in embedded application we often find a microcontroller but seldom a microprocessor.  The arithmetic power of microprocessors and microcontroller don?t give us a reason for a decision with processor we should select for a specific application. More interesting parts are the different interfaces and how difficult it is to connect external memories to a processor. With this features in our mind we will find good reason for our choice.

During the lecture we are working on import families for 8- and 16-bit microcontrollers. First we will have a short introduction in the hardware von these controllers. After this step we are going to fix the register layout of these controllers. This gives us a good basis to do the first assembler programs. Then we continue with proper C-programs and the combination between assembler- and C-programs. After the development of these programs we are go to our lab and try to implement and test these programs.

To prepare yourself for this lecture it is helpful if you have some knowledge in digital circuits. We need this knowledge when we try to build a microcontroller kit. For the programming steps it is also helpful if you have some basis knowledge in assembler programming (e.g. programming a 8086-processor) and in C-programming.
